Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about navigating appointments, insurance policies, and virtual triage.

How do I book an appointment on MediZap?

expand_more
Booking takes less than 60 seconds: search by doctor specialty or your preferred hospital, select an available date and time slot chip, confirm your contact details, and receive an instant SMS and calendar invitation.

What is the appointment cancellation policy?

expand_more
You can reschedule or cancel any appointment free of charge up to 2 hours prior to the scheduled time. If an upfront consultation deposit was charged, it is refunded automatically to your original payment method.

How are doctors verified on MediZap?

expand_more
Every clinical practitioner undergoes a rigorous 4-step credentialing audit verifying board registrations, hospital affiliations, state medical licenses, and peer malpractice history before listing.

Can I select between in-person and telehealth consultations?

expand_more
Yes. When selecting your slot, toggle between 'Clinic Visit' and 'Encrypted Video Call'. Telehealth consultations link directly into your secure MediZap patient portal with no third-party software required.

Where can I view my appointment history and clinical notes?

expand_more
Navigate to the 'My Appointments' tab in the top navigation bar. Here, you will find active passes, digital prescriptions, lab orders, and previous doctor consultation summaries stored under HIPAA compliance.

How does the MediZap AI Virtual Assistant work?

expand_more
MediZap AI leverages specialized clinical language models trained on symptom taxonomies. It parses natural conversational inputs (such as 'sharp back pain radiating down leg') and suggests the precise specialty, nearest accredited hospital, and instantly books the next opening.
